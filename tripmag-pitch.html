Team Discussion

Strategy & Architecture

PM
Product Manager
Market & MVP

Target: affluent millennial/Gen-Z travelers planning $5-15K international trips. They're paying travel agents $150-500/person — we undercut with better UX. MVP: itinerary + map + restaurants + packing. Price: $79-149/trip or $29/mo.

Biggest risk: AI hallucinating restaurants that don't exist. Mistakes cost real money and vacation days.

CT
CTO
Architecture & Infrastructure

Static generation is our moat. URL-based multi-tenancy, edge-deployed. Each trip: ~2MB static assets. Cost: $0.50-1.00 to generate, $0.01/mo to host. No databases at request time, no scaling nightmares.

Multi-stage AI pipeline with TypeScript compiler as quality gate. trip.json schema is the contract.

EN
Senior Engineer
Refactoring & Generalization

Core data model is already well-separated — biggest win. Need Zod schemas for runtime validation, component registry for conditional features, and dynamic map initialization. Japan-specific components (sakura, stamps, flashcards) become destination templates.

Performance concern: 500+ pins needs clustering, 100+ slides needs lazy loading. localStorage won't scale — need server-side state.

MK
Head of Marketing
Positioning & Growth

"Your Personal Trip Magazine, AI-Generated." Category: Experience Planners, not trip planners. Wanderlog/TripIt are digital clipboards — we're Vogue + National Geographic.

Viral loop: every trip page has a "Create Your Own" CTA. Launch: 20 micro-influencers → Reddit → ProductHunt. Target 5K waitlist pre-launch.

BD
Head of Sales
Revenue & Partnerships

The real money is B2B. Travel agencies need white-label SaaS: $5K-$50K annual contracts. Honeymoon planners, corporate travel ($30K-$200K). Sleeper opportunity: tourism boards at $50K-$500K/year.

Unit economics: $2-5 cost per trip. Agencies at $200/seat/month = $12-24K ACV, 70%+ gross margin. Year 1 target: $500K ARR from 50 customers.

Technical Architecture

Generation Pipeline

1

User Input

Multi-step wizard: destination, dates, travelers, dietary needs, budget, interests, travel style

2

AI Generation

Multi-stage LLM pipeline: outline → itinerary → hotels/restaurants → map pins → fun features. Incremental, not monolithic.

3

Validation

Zod schema validation, geocoding verification, URL health checks, budget consistency, pin-itinerary distance checks (<50km Haversine)

4

Deploy

Static Next.js build → Cloudflare Pages. Unique URL: tripmag.com/trip/[slug]. OG images via Satori. ~15-30s total build time.

Cost per trip: $2-5 (LLM + compute)  •  Hosting: $0.01/mo  •  Build: 15-30s
